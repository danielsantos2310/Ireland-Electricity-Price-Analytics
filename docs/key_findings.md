# Key findings

1. **A decade-level shift:** annual-average Irish business electricity prices rose about **70.3%** between 2015 and 2025.
2. **The shock was concentrated:** the semester peak was **28.40 c/kWh in 2022 S2**, then prices eased but did not return to the pre-crisis range.
3. **Gas and wholesale prices matter, but timing matters:** wholesale and gas movements help explain the crisis, while lagged wholesale features did not beat the naive retail forecast in out-of-sample testing.
4. **Customer scale matters:** in 2025 S2 Band IG paid **15.9 c/kWh**, materially below smaller-business bands and the weighted business average.
5. **Simplicity won validation:** over the broader 2018–2025 benchmark, the naive model had RMSE ≈ **2.43 c/kWh**, better than Holt and the tested gas-augmented model.
6. **2035 is a range, not a point:** weighted-average business prices in real 2025 money span roughly **21.2–30.1 c/kWh** across the three scenarios, with a central path near **24.4 c/kWh**.
